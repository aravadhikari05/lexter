B16.2 Short Form Citation (p.25)
================================

After providing a full citation of a periodical, you should use "<u>id.</u>" or "<u>supra</u>" in subsequent citations. Use "<u>id.</u>" to refer to periodical material cited in the immediately preceding citation. Otherwise, use the "<u>supra</u>" form introduced in [Bluepages B15.2](b15_2_short_form_citation.md).

Bluepages Tip:
--------------

When your document includes citations of more than one source by the same author, include an abbreviated reference to the title of the cited source in any <u>supra</u> citation:

Llewellyn, <u>Remarks</u>, <u>supra</u>, at 401--06.
