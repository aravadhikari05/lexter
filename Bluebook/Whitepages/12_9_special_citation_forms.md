12.9 Special Citation Forms (pp. 135--39)
========================================

12.9.1 -- Internal Revenue Code
------------------------------

In citations to the Internal Revenue Code, "26 U.S.C." may be replaced with "I.R.C." Thus:

26 U.S.C. § 61.

becomes:

I.R.C. § 61.

Citations to the Internal Revenue Code as it appears in an unofficial code should identify the unofficial code by placing the publisher's name in the parenthetical phrase containing, if included, the year of the version cited. Thus, citations to U.S.C.A. should appear:

I.R.C. § 1371 (West Supp. 1991).

I.R.C. § 1247 (West).

See also [rule 12.10](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-10-short-forms-for-statutes) regarding short form citation of statutes. For special citation forms for federal tax materials in court documents and legal memoranda, see [Bluepages B12.1.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/bluepages/b12-statutes-rules-and-restatements/b12-1-full-citation).

12.9.2 -- Ordinances
-------------------

Cite ordinances analogously to statutes. Always give the name of the political subdivision (such as a city or county) and the abbreviated state name at the beginning of the citation. Do not abbreviate the name of the political subdivision unless it is abbreviated in [table T10](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t10-geographical-terms). If the ordinance is codified, give the name of the code (abbreviated according to [table T1](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t1-united-states-jurisdictions)), the section or other subdivision, and the year of the code (determined according to [rule 12.3.2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-3-current-official-and-unofficial-codes)). Print the political subdivision, state, and code names in small capitals:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Montgomery, Ala., Code</span> § 3A-11 (1971).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Portland, Or., Police Code</span> art. 30 (1933).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Ft. Wor., Tex., Rev. Ordinances</span> ch. 34, art. I, § 15 (1950).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">S.F., Cal., Police Code</span> art. 16, div. 1, § 1076(a) (2000).

If the ordinance is uncodified, give its number (or, if none, its name) and, in a parenthetical, the exact date of adoption. Print the political subdivision, state, and ordinance name in ordinary roman type:

S.J., Cal., Ordinance 16,043 (Jan. 17, 1972).

Halifax County, Va., Ordinance to Regulate the Solicitation of Membership in Organizations (Aug. 6, 1956).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(</span>b<span style="font-variant: small-caps">)(6).</span>

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Fed. R. App.</span> P. 2.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">1st Cir. R. 6(</span>a<span style="font-variant: small-caps">).</span>

When citing rules no longer in force, give the most recent official source in which they appear and indicate the date of repeal parenthetically:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Sup. Ct. R.</span> 8, 306 U.S. 690 (1939) (repealed 1954).

12.9.4 -- Model Codes, Principles, Restatements, Standards, Sentencing Guidelines, and Uniform Acts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cite model codes, principles, restatements, standards, sentencing guidelines, uniform acts, and similar materials in small capitals, by section, rule, or other relevant subdivision. For restatements, give the year in which the principle or restatement was published. For model codes, principles, standards, and sentencing guidelines, give the year in which the code, standards, or guidelines were adopted. In that case, give the year of the last amendment, even when citing a portion not amended at that time. Usually the cover or title page of the source will indicate the date of the most recent amendments incorporated. When naming the code, principles, restatement, standards, or guidelines use the abbreviations listed in [table T6](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t6-case-names-and-institutional-authors-in-citations) (abbreviation of case names and periodicals) or suggested by the source itself. Indicate the author's name parenthetically. Abbreviate the author's name according to [rule 15.1(c)](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/15-books-reports-and-other-nonperiodic-materials/15-1-author) (institutional authors):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Principles of the L. of Fam. Dissolution: Analysis and Recommendations § 2.07 (A.L.I. 2002)</span>.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Third) of Unfair Competition § 3 (A.L.I. 1995)</span>.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual § 2D1.1(</span>c<span style="font-variant: small-caps">) (U.S. Sent'g Comm'n 2021).</span>

When citing to a uniform act itself, and not as the law of a particular state, cite it as a separate code. Indicate the author's name parenthetically. Abbreviate the author's name according to [rule 15.1(c)](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/15-books-reports-and-other-nonperiodic-materials/15-1-author) [](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v21/rules/15-books-reports-and-other-nonperiodic-materials/15-1-author#b-319662) (institutional authors):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.C.C. § 2-314 (A.L.I. & Unif. L. Comm'n 1977).</span>

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Unif. Tr. Code § 105 (Unif. L. Comm'n 2000).</span>

When citing a uniform act to the *Uniform Laws Annotated* (U.L.A.), provide the title of the act using abbreviations in [table T6](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t6-case-names-and-institutional-authors-in-citations), the section number, the year of amendment or repeal (if any), the appropriate volume of the U.L.A., the page number on which the relevant section appears, and the year of publication:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Unif. Adoption Act</span> § 10, 9 U.L.A. 45 (1988).

Give the year in which the uniform act was last amended, even if the section referred to was not amended at that time. If a uniform act or section has been withdrawn, superseded, or amended, indicate that fact parenthetically according to [rule 12.7](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-7-invalidation-repeal-amendment-and-prior-history):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Unif. Prob. Code</span> § 2-706 (amended 2020), 8 pt. 1 U.L.A. 291 (2013).

When citing to the law of a particular state, cite as a separate code:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Okla. Stat</span>. tit. 12A, § 2-314 (2004).

If a code, principle, restatement, or set of standards or guidelines is a tentative or proposed draft, indicate that fact parenthetically as it appears on the publication and give the draft number (if available) and the year of the draft:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Land Dev. Code § 2-402(2)</span> (A.L.I.<span style="font-variant: small-caps"></span>, Proposed Official Draft 1975).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Second) of Torts</span> § 847A (A.L.I.<span style="font-variant: small-caps"></span>, Tentative Draft No. 17, 1974).

If a restatement contains a subtitle, retain the subtitle in the citation:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Third) of Prop.: Wills and Donative Transfers § 2.1 (A.L.I. 1999).</span>

When citing a draft of a uniform act, indicate the fact parenthetically and give the date of the draft:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Elec. Wills Act</span> (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Unif. L. Comm'n</span>, Draft Jan. 22, 2019).

Cite Generally Accepted Auditing Standards and Generally Accepted Accounting Principles as follows:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Codification of Acct. Standards & Procs.</span>, Statement on Auditing Standards No. 1, § 150 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Am. Inst. of Certified Pub. Accts.</span> 1972).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Rsch. and Dev. Arrangements</span>, Statement of Fin. Acct. Standards No. 68, § 32 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Fin. Acct. Standards Bd.</span> 1982).

Comments, notes, and other addenda should be cited according to [rule 3.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/3-subdivisions/3-4-appended-material):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Penal Code</span> § 223.6 note on status of section (A.L.I<span style="font-variant: small-caps">.,</span> Proposed Official Draft 1962).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Second) of Conflict of L.</span> § 305 cmt. b, illus. 1 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps"></span>A.L.I. 1977).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Standards for Traffic Just.</span> § 4.2 cmt. at 9 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.</span> 1975).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Unif. Prudent Inv. Act § 2</span> cmt. <span style="font-variant: small-caps">(Unif. L. Comm'n 1994).\
</span>

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Second) of Conts.</span> ch. 16, topic 3, intro. note (A.L.I. 1981).

Cite application notes, background commentary, introductory commentary, and appendices to sentencing guidelines as follows:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual</span> § 3D1.4 cmt. n.1 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Comm'n</span> 2021).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual</span> § 2D1.2 cmt. background (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Comm'n</span> 2021).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual</span> ch. 3, pt. D, introductory cmt. (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Comm'n</span> 2021).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Guidelines Manual</span> app. C (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Sent'g Comm'n</span> 2021).

When citing a version of a code, restatement, or set of standards that has been withdrawn or amended, indicate that fact according to [rules 12.7.2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-7-invalidation-repeal-amendment-and-prior-history) or [12.7.3](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-7-invalidation-repeal-amendment-and-prior-history):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Bus. Corp. Act</span> § 2(f) (1969) (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.,</span> amended 1973).

12.9.5 -- ABA Code of Professional Responsibility and Opinions on Ethics
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Cite the old *Model Code of Professional Responsibility* and the new *Model Rules of Professional Conduct* according to [rule 12.9.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/12-statutes/12-9-special-citation-forms):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Code of Pro. Resp.</span> Canon 2 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.</span> 1980).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Rules of Pro. Conduct</span> r. 3.12 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.,</span> Discussion Draft 1983).

Cite ethical considerations and disciplinary rules as follows:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Code of Pro. Resp.</span> EC 7-36 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A. </span>1980).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Code of Pro. Resp.</span> DR 8-101 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.</span> 1980).

Cite notes or other commentary according to [rule 3.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/3-subdivisions/3-4-appended-material):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Model Rules of Pro. Conduct</span> r. 1.15 cmt. (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.B.A.</span> 1983).

Cite formal and informal opinions of the Committee on Ethics and Professional Responsibility (or the older Committees on Professional Ethics (1958--1971) and on Professional Ethics and Grievances (1919--1958)) by issuing body, opinion number, and year. Abbreviate according to [table T6](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t6-case-names-and-institutional-authors-in-citations):

A.B.A. Comm. on Pro. Ethics & Grievances, Formal Op. 35 (1931).

A.B.A. Comm. on Ethics & Pro. Resp., Informal Op. 1414 (1978).

The subject of the opinion may be given parenthetically:

A.B.A. Comm. on Ethics & Pro. Resp., Formal Op. 338 (1974) (discussing the use of credit cards for the payment of legal services and expenses).
