3.4 Appended Material (p.82)
============================

Indicate an appendix or appended note or comment by placing the appropriate abbreviation (see [table T16](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t16-subdivisions)) after the citation to the largest full subdivision to which the item is appended, whether page, section, paragraph, chapter, title, or volume:

James Edwin Kee & Terrence A. Moan, Comment, *The Property Tax and Tenant Equality*, 89 <span style="font-variant: small-caps">Harv. L. Rev.</span> 531 app. (1976).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Second) of Torts</span> § 623A cmt. a (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.L.I.</span> 1977).

Cite a particular page, section, or other subdivision in an appendix as follows:

50 U.S.C. app. § 454.

Samuel Issacharoff & George Loewenstein, *Second Thoughts About Summary Judgment*, 100 <span style="font-variant: small-caps">Yale L.J.</span> 73 app. at 124--25 (1990).

Other types of appended material that serve as commentary on the material to which they are appended, or that further discuss a point related to the textual discussion, should be cited as notes or appendices:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Fed. R. Civ. P.</span> 60(b) advisory committee's note to 1946 amendment.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Restatement (Second) of Prop.</span> § 2.1 cmt. c, illus. 2 (<span style="font-variant: small-caps">A.L.I. </span>1977).

Further information necessary to identify which of several named notes is cited may be added parenthetically:

42 U.S.C. § 1862 note (1988) (Denial of Financial Assistance to Campus Disrupters).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">N.Y. Bus. Corp. Law</span> § 624 note (McKinney 1963) (Legislative Studies and Reports).

Appendices that reprint materials normally cited to another source should be cited according to [rule 1.6(a)](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/1-structure-and-use-of-citations/1-6-related-authority):

An Act for the Prevention of Frauds and Perjuries, 1677, 29 Car. 2, c. 2, § 17 (Eng.), *reprinted in* <span style="font-variant: small-caps">John P. Dawson, William Burnett Harvey & Stanley D. Henderson, Cases and Comments on Contracts</span> app. 1, at 942 (5th ed. 1988).
