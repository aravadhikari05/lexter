T10 Geographical Terms (p.340)
==============================

The following list provides abbreviations for geographical locations for use in case citations ([rules 10.2.2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/10-cases/10-2-case-names) and [10.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/10-cases/10-4-court-and-jurisdiction)), names of institutional authors ([rule 15.1(c)](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/15-books-reports-and-other-nonperiodic-materials/15-1-author)), periodical abbreviations ([rule 16](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/16-periodical-materials) and [table T13](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t13-periodicals)), foreign materials ([rule 20](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/20-foreign-materials/20-1-jurisdiction)), and treaty citations ([rule 21.4.2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/21-international-materials/21-4-treaties-and-other-international-agreements)).

<br>

Any countries, territories, provinces, or cities omitted from [tables T10.2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t10-geographical-terms/t10-2-australian-states-and-canadian-provinces-and-territories) and [T10.3](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t10-geographical-terms/t10-3-countries-and-regions) should not be abbreviated, except according to rules otherwise laid out in [table T6](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t6-case-names-and-institutional-authors-in-citations).
