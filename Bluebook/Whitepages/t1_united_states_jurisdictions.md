T1 United States Jurisdictions (p.257)
======================================

The abbreviations and citation conventions listed in this table, except as noted, are primarily intended to serve a national audience. Practitioners should also adhere to local citation rules (see [Bluepages table BT2](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/bluetables/bt2-jurisdiction-specific-citation-rules-and-style-guides)). The preferred sources of citation are suggested when relevant. In the absence of such preference, the user may cite to any listed sources, as appropriate. In general, when citing to online sources, be sure to cite to stable URLs of the specific provision cited in accordance with the principles of [rule 18](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/18-the-internet-electronic-media-and-other-nonprint-resources).
