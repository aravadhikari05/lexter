T8 Explanatory Phrases (p.339)
==============================

The following table lists a number of explanatory phrases (some of which contain abbreviations) commonly used to indicate prior or subsequent history and weight of authority of judicial decisions. Explanatory phrases that take a direct object---typically phrases ending in a preposition or a verbal "-ing"---are not followed by commas. All other explanatory phrases are followed by commas, which are *not* italicized. See [rule 10.7](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/10-cases/10-7-prior-and-subsequent-history) for guidance in using explanatory phrases.
