B18.2 Short Form Citation (p.28)
================================

After providing a full citation to an internet source, you may use "<u>id.</u>" or "<u>supra</u>" in subsequent citations per [rules 4](../Whitepages/4_short_citation_forms.md) and [18.12](../Whitepages/18_9_short_citation_forms.md):

Lat, <u>supra</u>.
