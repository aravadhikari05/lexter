# Case Names — Condensed Rules

## B10.1.1 — Case Names (Bluepages)

- **(i)** Omit all parties other than the first on each side of "v."
  - *Dow Jones & Co. v. Harrods, Ltd.*
- **(ii)** For individuals, use only last names; omit first names, middle names, initials
  - *Darwin v. Dawkins*
- **(iii)** Omit words indicating multiple parties ("et al.") and alternative names ("a.k.a.")
  - *Kant v. Bentham*
- **(iv)** Procedural phrases:
  - Abbreviate "in the matter of," "petition of," etc. to "In re"
  - Abbreviate "on the relation of," "on behalf of," etc. to "ex rel."
  - When adversary parties are named, omit all procedural phrases except "ex rel."
  - *Dombroski ex rel. Estate of Dombroski v. Chi. Park Dist.*
  - *In re Fairfax*
- **(v)** Abbreviate words listed in T6 (below), unless in a textual sentence (see (vi))
  - Abbreviate states/countries/geographical units per T10 unless the unit is a named party
  - Never abbreviate "United States" when it is a named party
  - Omit "The" as first word of party name (unless object of in rem action)
  - May abbreviate words of 8+ letters if space saved and result unambiguous
  - May abbreviate entities with widely recognized initials (NAACP, FCC, NLRB)
  - *Seattle Times v. Univ. of Wash.*
  - *Laidlaw Corp. v. NLRB*
- **(vi)** Textual sentence rule: when case name appears in running text (not a citation sentence), only abbreviate widely known acronyms and these 8 words: &, Ass'n, Bros., Co., Corp., Inc., Ltd., No.
  - *In Penn Central Transportation Co. v. City of New York, 366 N.E.2d 1271 (N.Y. 1977), the court applied...*
  - Subsequent references: may use one party's name if unambiguous (*Penn Central*)
- Underline/italicize the entire case name up to but not including the comma that follows it

## Rule 10.2.1 — General Rules for Case Names

### (a) Actions and parties cited
- If consolidation of two or more actions, cite only the first listed
- Omit all parties other than the first listed on each side
- Do not omit the first-listed relator or any portion of a partnership name
  - *Eisen v. Spradlin, Lincoln & Amorosi*
- Omit "et al." and alternative names (d/b/a, etc.)
- In rem: omit all but first-listed item; use common street address for real property
  - *United States v. 6109 Grubb Road*
- Bankruptcy: if both adversary and nonadversary names appear, cite adversary first, nonadversary in parentheses with "In re"
  - *Wallingford's, Inc. v. Waning (In re Waning), 120 B.R. 607, 611 (Bankr. D. Me. 1990).*

### (b) Procedural phrases
- "on the relation of," "for the use of," "on behalf of," "as next friend of" => "*ex rel.*"
- "in the matter of," "petition of," "application of" => "*In re*"
- Omit all procedural phrases except the first; when adversary parties named, omit all except "*ex rel.*"
- Include descriptive phrases: "Accounting of," "Estate of," "Will of"
  - *In re Will of Holt*
  - *Estate of Haas v. Commissioner*

### (c) Abbreviations (textual sentences)
- Only abbreviate widely known acronyms and these 8 words: &, Ass'n, Bros., Co., Corp., Inc., Ltd., No.
- Do not abbreviate if one of these 8 begins a party's name

### (d) "The"
- Omit "The" as first word of party name, except:
  - Object of in rem action: *In re The Clinton Bridge*
  - "The King" / "The Queen": *The King v. Broadrup*
  - Established popular names in citations: *See The Civil Rights Cases, 109 U.S. 3 (1883).*
  - But omit "The" when referring to case textually: *the Civil Rights Cases*

### (e) Descriptive terms
- Omit "administrator," "appellee," "executor," "licensee," "trustee" when describing a party already named
  - *Burns v. McMillen* (not "Burns v. McMillen, Administrator")
- Retain when the term is part of the party identity: *Trustees of Dartmouth College v. Woodward*

### (f) Geographical terms
- Omit "State of," "Commonwealth of," "People of" — except when citing that state's own courts, then retain "State," "Commonwealth," or "People"
  - *Commonwealth v. Ferrone, 448 A.2d 637 (Pa. Super. Ct. 1982).*
- Omit "City of," "County of," etc. unless it begins a party name
  - *Butts v. City of New York*
- Omit prepositional phrases of location not following "City" etc., unless it would leave only one word or is part of full business name
  - *Planned Parenthood of Southeastern Pennsylvania v. Casey*
- Include national/larger geographical designations (except in union names); omit "of America" after "United States"
- Retain geographical designations not introduced by a preposition
- Omit all geographical designations that follow a comma
  - *City of Arlington v. FCC* (not *City of Arlington, Texas v. FCC*)

### (g) Given names or initials
- Omit given names/initials of individuals; retain in business firm names
- Do not omit any part of a multi-word surname: *Van der Velt v. Standing Horse*
- Retain given names that follow a surname (Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese names): *Yao Zhen Guang v. Yeh Zhi An*
- Spanish/Portuguese names: cite surname and all names following: *Ortega y Gasset v. Alcala de Larosa*

### (h) Business firm designations
- Omit "Inc.," "Ltd.," "L.L.C.," "N.A.," "F.S.B." if name already contains "Ass'n," "Bros.," "Co.," "Corp.," "Ins.," or "R.R." (indicating business entity)
  - *Wisconsin Packing Co. v. Indiana Refrigerator Lines, Inc.* (not *Wisconsin Packing Co., Inc.*)
- Read narrowly: only omit if no possible confusion with a natural person

### (i) Union and local union names
- Cite exactly as given, subject to:
  - (i) Cite only the smallest unit
  - (ii) Omit all craft/industry designations except the first full designation
  - (iii) Widely recognized abbreviations (e.g., UAW) permitted
  - (iv) Omit all prepositional phrases of location

### (j) Commissioner of Internal Revenue
- Cite as "Commissioner" or in citations "Comm'r"

### (k) Common names different from reporter name
- If case known by a common name not in reporter: may substitute entirely or indicate parenthetically
  - *The Prize Cases, 67 U.S. (2 Black) 635 (1863).*
- If known by reporter name but also has a short common name: indicate parenthetically in italics (cannot substitute)
  - *Youngstown Sheet & Tube Co. v. Sawyer (Steel Seizure), 343 U.S. 579 (1952).*
- Multiple dispositions identifier: *Hamdi v. Rumsfeld (Hamdi III), 316 F.3d 450 (4th Cir. 2003).*

## Rule 10.2.2 — Additional Rules for Case Names in Citations

- Always abbreviate any word listed in T6, even if first word of party's name — UNLESS the word is part of a state/country/geographical unit that is the entire name of the party
  - *S. Consol. R.R. v. Consol. Transp. Co.* — but *South Dakota v. Dole* (not *S. Dakota v. Dole*)
- Abbreviate states/countries/geographical units per T10 unless the unit is the entire name of the party (as opposed to part thereof). This includes "United States."
  - *Ctr. for Nat'l Sec. Stud. v. U.S. Dep't of Just.* — but *Alvarez-Machain v. United States*
  - *Fritz v. County of Los Angeles* (geographical unit is entire party name)
- Abbreviate other words of 8+ letters if substantial space saved and result unambiguous
- When textual sentence already mentions case name in full, case name may be omitted from citation
