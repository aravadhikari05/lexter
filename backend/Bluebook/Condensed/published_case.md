# Published Cases — Reporters, Courts, Jurisdiction

## B10.1.2 — Reporters and Pinpoint Citations (Bluepages)

- Cite a reporter by listing: (1) volume number, (2) abbreviated reporter name (per T1), (3) first page of case report
- **Pinpoint citation (pincite):** place after the first page, separated by a comma
- **Page range:** first and last page separated by en dash/hyphen; for 3+ digit numbers, drop repetitious digits except final two (e.g., 102--06; 1020--30)
- **Multiple nonconsecutive pages:** list each page/range separated by commas (e.g., 103, 106--08, 132)
- **Passim:** use in lieu of pincite when material spans too many nonconsecutive pages
- **Footnote cite:** page number + "n." + footnote number, no space (e.g., 199 n.4)
- If pincite is the first page of the case, repeat the page number
  - *Baker v. Carr, 369 U.S. 186, 195 (1962).*
  - *Newdow v. U.S. Cong., 328 F.3d 466, 471 n.3 (9th Cir. 2003).*
  - *United States v. Baxter, 492 F.2d 150, 150 (9th Cir. 1973).*

## B10.1.3 — Court and Year of Decision (Bluepages)

- Indicate court and year of decision parenthetically, immediately after the page reference
- For U.S. Supreme Court: do NOT include court name

### (i) United States Supreme Court
- Cite United States Reports (U.S.) if available; otherwise Supreme Court Reporter (S. Ct.)
  - *Meritor Sav. Bank v. Vinson, 477 U.S. 57, 60 (1986).*
  - *Weyerhaeuser Co. v. U.S. Fish & Wildlife Serv., 139 S. Ct. 361, 365 (2018).*

### (ii) Federal Courts of Appeals
- Cite Federal Reporter (F., F.2d, F.3d, F.4th); indicate court parenthetically
  - *Env't Def. Fund v. EPA, 465 F.2d 528, 533 (D.C. Cir. 1972).*
  - *United States v. Jardine, 364 F.3d 1200, 1203 (10th Cir. 2004).*

### (iii) Federal District Courts
- Cite Federal Supplement (F. Supp., F. Supp. 2d, F. Supp. 3d); indicate court parenthetically
  - *W. St. Grp. L.L.C. v. Epro, 563 F. Supp. 2d 84, 91 (D. Mass. 2008).*

### (iv) State High Courts
- Cite regional reporter if available; otherwise official state reporter (per T1.3)
- Indicate state parenthetically unless unambiguously conveyed by reporter title
  - *People v. Armour, 590 N.W.2d 61 (Mich. 1999).*
  - *Bates v. Tappan, 99 Mass. 376 (1868).* (state clear from reporter)

### (v) Other State Courts
- Cite regional reporter if available; otherwise official state reporter (per T1.3)
- Indicate state AND court parenthetically unless unambiguously conveyed by reporter title
- Do NOT indicate department or district of intermediate state courts
  - *Lundman v. McKown, 530 N.W.2d 807 (Minn. Ct. App. 1995).*
  - *Campbell v. Parker-Hannifin Corp., 82 Cal. Rptr. 2d 202 (Ct. App. 1999).*

### Parallel Citation (state court documents)
- Follow local rules; sometimes requires both official + unofficial reporter
- Include pincite for each reporter
- When state/court clear from official reporter, omit from date parenthetical
  - *Pledger v. Halvorson, 324 Ark. 302, 921 S.W.2d 576 (1996).*

### State abbreviation spacing rules
- No space between adjacent single capital letters: S.D.N.Y.
- Space between single capital letter and longer abbreviation: D. Conn., S.D. Cal.

## Rule 10.3 — Reporters and Other Sources

### 10.3.1 — Parallel Citations and Which Source(s) to Cite

#### (a) State court documents
- Cite source(s) required by local rules (often official reporter + regional reporter in parallel)

#### (b) All other documents (memoranda, law review)
- Cite the regional reporter if decision found therein
  - *Swedloff v. Phila. Transp. Co., 187 A.2d 152 (Pa. 1963).*
- If available as official public domain citation, provide that + parallel regional reporter cite
- If not in regional reporter or public domain: cite other sources per T1
- Preference order for unavailable cases: unofficial reporter > electronic database > service > slip opinion > internet source > newspaper

### 10.3.2 — Reporters
- Citation format: volume + abbreviated reporter name + first page
- Early American reporters named after editors; cite by official series name, not editor name
  - *Cobb v. Davenport, 32 N.J.L. 369 (Sup. Ct. 1867).* (not *3 Vroom 369*)
- For U.S. Supreme Court through 90 U.S. (23 Wall.) and some early state reporters: give editor name and volume in parentheses
  - *Green v. Biddle, 21 U.S. (8 Wheat.) 1 (1823).*
  - *Hall v. Bell, 47 Mass. (6 Met.) 431 (1843).*
- If pagination differs between reprint and original: give parallel citations
  - *Wadsworth v. Ruggles, 23 Mass. 62, 6 Pick. 63 (1828).*

### 10.3.3 — Public Domain Format
- When available in public domain (medium-neutral) format: case name, year of decision, state two-character postal code, T7 court abbreviation (omit if highest court), sequential decision number, "U" suffix if unpublished
- Pincite to paragraph number
- Provide parallel citation to regional reporter if available
  - *Beck v. Beck, 1999 ME 110, para. 6, 733 A.2d 981, 983.*
  - *Gregory v. Class, 1998 SD 106, para. 3, 584 N.W.2d 873, 875.*
  - *Jones v. Fisher, 1998 OK Civ. App. 120U.*
- If jurisdiction uses a different format, follow that format
  - *Cannon v. Am. Bowling Cong., 94-0647, p. 1 (La. 4/29/94), 637 So. 2d 463, 463.*

## Rule 10.4 — Court and Jurisdiction

- Every citation must indicate the deciding court
- Give court name + geographical jurisdiction (per T1/T2, T7, T10) in parenthetical with date
- Format ordinals per Rule 6.2(b)

### (a) Federal courts
- **U.S. Supreme Court:** omit court name when citing U.S. Reports or S. Ct.; use "U.S." when citing U.S. Law Week
- **Circuit Justice sitting on lower court:** cite as *(Thomas, Circuit Justice)*
- **Courts of Appeals (numbered):** *2d Cir.* (not C.C.A.2d or CA2)
- **D.C. Circuit / Federal Circuit:** *D.C. Cir.*, *Fed. Cir.*
- **District courts:** give district, not division: *D.N.J.*, *D.D.C.*, *C.D. Cal.* (not *C.D. Cal. E.D.*)
- **Old circuit courts (abolished 1912):** *C.C.S.D.N.Y.*, *C.C.E.D. Mo.*
- **Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation:** *J.P.M.L.*
- **FISA courts:** *F.I.S.C.*, *F.I.S.C.R.*
- **Bankruptcy:** *Bankr. E.D. Va.*, *B.A.P. 9th Cir.*

### (b) State courts
- Indicate state and court of decision
- Do NOT include court name if it is the highest court of the state
  - *People v. Armour, 590 N.W.2d 61 (Mich. 1999).* (not *Mich. Sup. Ct.*)
- Omit jurisdiction/court if unambiguously conveyed by reporter title
  - *DiLucia v. Mandelker, 493 N.Y.S.2d 769 (App. Div. 1985).* (not *N.Y. App. Div.*)
  - *Bates v. Tappan, 99 Mass. 376 (1868).* (neither court nor state needed)
- Do NOT indicate department, district, or county unless particularly relevant
  - If relevant: *Schiffman v. Corsi, 50 N.Y.S.2d 897 (Sup. Ct. N.Y. Cnty. 1944).*
