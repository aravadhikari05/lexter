# Unpublished / Pending Cases — Condensed Rules

## B10.1.4 — Pending and Unreported Cases (Bluepages)

### (i) LexisNexis and Westlaw cases
- Format: (1) case name; (2) docket number; (3) database identifier + electronic report number; (4) star page number; (5) court and full date parenthetical
- Cite docket number exactly as it appears; if multiple docket numbers, omit lead-in language (e.g., "No.," "Civ.") after first reference
  - *Albrecht v. Stranczek, No. 87 C 9535, 1991 U.S. Dist. LEXIS 5088, at \*1, \*3 (N.D. Ill. Apr. 15, 1991).*
  - *Brown v. Henderson Cnty. Sheriff's Off., No. 1:23-cv-00270-MR-WCM, 2024 LX 18483, at \*1 (W.D.N.C. May 14, 2024).*
  - *Kvaas Constr. Co. v. United States, No. 90-266C, 1991 WL 47632, at \*2--3 (Cl. Ct. Apr. 8, 1991).*
  - *PKFinans Int'l Corp. v. IBJ Schroder Leasing Corp., Nos. 93 Civ. 5375, 96 Civ. 1816 (SAS) (HBP), 1996 WL 525862 (S.D.N.Y. Sept. 17, 1996).*

### (ii) Slip opinions
- When unreported but available as slip opinion: docket number, court, and full date of most recent major disposition
  - *Groucho Marx Prods. v. Playboy Enters., No. 77 Civ. 1782 (S.D.N.Y. Dec. 30, 1977).*

### (iii) Opinions only available online (not in electronic database)
- Include the webpage URL at end of citation
  - *Kaye v. Trump, No. 5128, slip op. at 1 (N.Y. App. Div. Jan. 29, 2009), <http://www.nycourts.gov/reporter/3dseries/2009/2009_00452.htm>.*

## Rule 10.8.1 — Pending and Unreported Cases

### (a) Cases available on electronic media
- When unreported but on a widely used database: provide case name, docket number, database identifier, court name, full date of most recent major disposition
- Cite docket number as on court documents; may omit initial digit before colon and judge's initials at end
- If database has unique identifiers (LexisNexis, Westlaw, Bloomberg Law), include them
- Screen/page numbers preceded by asterisk; paragraph numbers preceded by paragraph symbol
  - *Int'l Snowmobile Mfrs. Ass'n v. Norton, No. 00-CV-229-B, 2004 WL 2337372, at \*3, \*7 (D. Wyo. Oct. 14, 2004).*
  - *Shelton v. City of Manhattan Beach, No. B171606, 2004 WL 2163741, at \*1 (Cal. Ct. App. Sep. 28, 2004).*
  - *Ortho-McNeil Pharm., Inc. v. Teva Pharm. Indus., Ltd., No. 2008-1549, 2009 BL 181480, at \*4 (Fed. Cir. Aug. 26, 2009).*
  - *Gibbs v. Frank, No. 02-3924, 2004 U.S. App. LEXIS 21357, at \*18--19 (3d Cir. Oct. 14, 2004).*
- LexisNexis 2024 alternative short form:
  - *Albrecht v. Stranczek, No. 87 C 9535, 1991 LX 2672345, at \*1, \*3 (N.D. Ill. Apr. 15, 1991).*
- If database name not clear from identifier, include parenthetically:
  - *Staats v. Brown, No. 65681-9, 2000 WA 0042007, para. 25 (Wash. Jan. 6, 2000) (VersusLaw).*
- If no unique database identifier: include specific collection info:
  - *Frankel v. Banco Nacional de Mex., S.A., No. 82 Civ. 6547 (S.D.N.Y. May 31, 1983) (LEXIS, Genfed Library, Dist. File).*

### (b) Cases available in slip opinions
- Give docket number, court, full date of most recent major disposition
  - *Groucho Marx Prods. v. Playboy Enters., No. 77 Civ. 1782 (S.D.N.Y. Dec. 30, 1977).*
- Note any renumbering of docket
- Always give the full docket number (No. 75-31, not No. 31)
- If date is not the decision date: indicate significance in parenthetical
  - *Ross v. Weissman, No. 90-345 (D. Mass. filed Sep. 18, 1990).*
- Pincite to separately paginated slip opinion:
  - *Ross v. Weissman, No. 90-345, slip op. at 6 (D. Mass. Dec. 4, 1990).*
- If not separately paginated, cite beginning page + cited page:
  - *Bedell v. Nessim, No. 90-567, slip op. 3458, 3465 (1st Cir. Jan. 19, 1991).*
- Bankruptcy adversary proceedings: supply both case number and adversary number:
  - *Brown v. Sachs (In re Brown), Ch. 7 Case No. 84-00170-G, Adv. No. 85-1190, slip op. at 5 (E.D. Mich. Jan. 24, 1986).*

### (c) Other pending and unreported cases
- May cite to services, periodicals, or the internet

### (d) Depublished cases
- Indicate parenthetically when no reported order:
  - *Mitchell v. Cal. Fair Plan Ass'n, 260 Cal. Rptr. 3 (Ct. App. 1989) (depublished).*
- If depublishing order is reported, indicate as subsequent history with "*depublished by,*"
