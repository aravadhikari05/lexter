11 Constitutions (pp. 124--25)
=============================

Citation of Section 2 of the Fourteenth Amendment to the U.S. Constitution:

![Image of citation of Section 2 of the Fourteenth Amendment to the U.S. Constitution](https://assets.legalbluebook.com/guide/rule11_21.png)

Generally, cite constitutions by the abbreviated name of the jurisdiction (as indicated in [table T10](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t10-geographical-terms)) and the word "<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Const</span>." Constitutions of nongovernmental entities should also be abbreviated according to [table T6](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t6-case-names-and-institutional-authors-in-citations). Abbreviate the subdivisions of constitutions, such as articles and clauses, according to [table T16](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/tables/t16-subdivisions):

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. art. I, § 9, cl. 2.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. amend. XIV, § 2.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const.</span> pmbl.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const.</span> art. III, §§ 1--2.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">W. Va. Const.</span> art. I, § I.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">World Health Org. Const.</span> ch<span style="font-variant: small-caps">. 2, </span>art. II. 

Follow the numbering convention of each individual constitution:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Iowa Const.</span> art. I, § 1.

But also:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">La. Const.</span> art. X, pt. IV.

Cite constitutional provisions currently in force without a date. If the cited provision has been repealed, either indicate parenthetically the fact and year of repeal or cite the repealing provision in full:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. amend. XVIII (repealed 1933).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. amend. XVIII, *repealed by*, <span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. amend. XXI.

When citing a provision that has been subsequently amended, either indicate parenthetically the fact and year of amendment or cite the amending provision in full:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. art. I, § 3, cl. 1 (amended 1913).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. art. I, § 3, cl. 1, *amended by*, <span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const</span>. amend. XVII.

Cite constitutions that have been totally superseded or are otherwise no longer in effect by year of adoption; if the specific provision cited was adopted in a different year than the constitution, give that year parenthetically:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Articles of Confederation</span> of 1781, art. IX, para. 1.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Ark. Const.</span> of 1868, art. III, § 2 (1873).

When citing a constitution contained in an electronic database, indicate parenthetically the name of the publisher, editor, or compiler unless the constitution is published, edited, or compiled by or under the supervision of, federal or state officials. Also indicate the name of the database and information regarding the currentness of the database as provided by the database itself:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">Wash. Const</span>. art. I, § 2 (West, Westlaw through Nov. 2024 amendments).

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">P.R. Const.</span> art. II, § 1 (LexisNexis, LEXIS through June 2024). 

When referring to multiple amendments, sections within the same article, or clauses within the same section, it is permissible to use a single citation clause. Otherwise, the second citation should use "*id.*" rather than repeat the name of the constitution:

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const.</span> amends. V, XIV.

<span style="font-variant: small-caps">U.S. Const.</span> art. I, § 8; *id.* art. II, § 2.

Do not use a short citation form (other than "*id.*") for constitutions.

| | Citation | Text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. Constitution | U.S. <span style="font-variant: small-caps">Const.</span> art. IV, § 1 | Article IV, Section 1 or the Full Faith and Credit Clause |
| State constitution | S.C. <span style="font-variant: small-caps"><span style="font-variant: small-caps">Const</span></span>. art.I, § 12 | article I, section 12 or the double jeopardy clause |
| Nongovernmental organization constitution | <span style="font-variant: small-caps">World Health Org. Const.</span> ch. <span style="font-variant: small-caps">2,</span>art II, § (a) | chapter 2, article II, section (a) or coordination clause |

Special rules for foreign and tribal constitutions supersede this rule. For foreign constitutions, see [rule 20.4](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/20-foreign-materials/20-4-constitutions).  For tribal constitutions, see [rule 22.2.1](https://www.legalbluebook.com/bluebook/v22/rules/22-tribal-nations/22-2-tribal-nations-without-an-established-citation-format).
